# Reports

## Project Name

**AI-Powered Customer Support Ticketing System**

## Overview

**Salesforce Reports** are used to monitor, analyze, and track customer support activities and case management.

## Reports Created

### 1. Cases by Status

This report displays the number of customer support cases in each status:

- **New**
- **Working**
- **Escalated**
- **Closed**

### 2. Cases by Priority

This report shows the distribution of customer support cases based on their priority level:

- **High**
- **Medium**
- **Low**

### 3. Case Owner Report

This report lists the cases assigned to each **support agent**, helping managers monitor workload distribution and case ownership.

## Benefits

The reports provide the following benefits:

- **Monitor support team performance**.
- **Identify high-priority issues**.
- **Track pending and resolved cases**.
- Support **better decision-making through analytics**.
- Provide improved **visibility into support operations**.

## Report Summary

| **Report** | **Purpose** |
|---|---|
| Cases by Status | Monitor cases based on their current status |
| Cases by Priority | Analyze the distribution of case priorities |
| Case Owner Report | Track cases assigned to individual support agents |
